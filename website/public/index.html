What it does

Built for the list you actually share

Six things EveryList does well, instead of a hundred things behind a subscription.

Real-time, for real

Add milk on your phone, watch it land on theirs before you've put yours down — every list is shared live.

Keeps working offline

Check things off in a dead zone at the store. Everything queues up and syncs the moment you're back.

Learns your aisles

Pick your store and the list sorts itself to match its layout — it gets smarter about your items over time.

A color for every list

Groceries, the hardware store, Saturday's BBQ — each list gets its own color and icon so you spot it instantly.

Run it yourself. Keep it yours.

EveryList ships as a single self-hosted container. Stand it up on your own hardware or a small VPS, point the app at it, and every list, item, and photo stays on infrastructure you own — never on ours, because we don't run one.

  • $ docker compose up
  • → one container, your data
  • → no account with us, ever
